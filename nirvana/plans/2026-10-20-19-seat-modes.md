## 6. The modes

### 6.1 Efficient (L2)

- **Bar:** the class's efficient bar.
- **Rank:** pressure, the rule's usage cost (§2's numbers), then score, then the class's order. With the recalibrated
  table, quota projected to go unused counts at `spare_weight`, so projected-spare Gemini quota is spent first.
- **Effort:** `efficient_effort`, the lowest listed for the class.
- **The step down:** as today, one effort lower when a priced skip left no seat.

### 6.2 Balanced (L3)

- **The climb:** for the class's strongest seat that survives the filters, its pool's
  `climb = max over its windows of (used + booked) / max(elapsed, pace_ease)`, the projected share at reset.
- **The bar drops with the climb:**
  `t = clamp((climb - climb_start) / (climb_full - climb_start), 0, 1)`, and the bar is
  `balanced_bar - t * (balanced_bar - efficient_bar)`. New constants `climb_start = 0.70` and `climb_full = 1.00`,
  both hypotheses.
- **Effort steps down with the climb:** at `t >= 0.5`, the seats of the hot pool take one effort lower than
  `effort`, never under `efficient_effort`.
- **Rank:** pressure, as Efficient does, then score, then the class's order.

So at a cool climb only the strong seats clear the bar and the cheapest of them wins: opus for plan and load-bearing
work, sonnet for bounded work. As the strong seat's pool climbs, cheaper seats come in and win on pressure. On today's
readings the Max week projects to about 1.18, so `t = 1`, Max steps down an effort, and argon and flash compete at the
efficient bar.

### 6.3 SOTA (L4, L5)

- **Bar:** 100, the best seat left, as today.
- **Rank:** score, then the class's order. No pressure, no climb, no headroom filter (filter 6), no reset discount.
- **Effort:** `sota_effort`, capped at high.
- **Still in force:** a seat that is off, lacks a need, or whose pool is at the floor (5%), and the orchestrator's
  5 h reserve. An exhausted pool cannot run an order, and the reserve keeps the orchestrator able to answer. When one
  of those removes the best seat, the pick says so and seats the next by score.

### 6.4 The fallback rank (L8)

- A seat with `fallback = true` ranks after every survivor without it, in every mode: a tier above pressure and score.
  g-opus and g-sonnet carry it.
- Its score still counts for the bar, so it can take a class when it is the seat left.
- The reason line says `fallback: <why the others were skipped>`.
- Decision 0017's not-family pass is unchanged: g-opus is family anthropic.
