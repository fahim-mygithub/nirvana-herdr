## What was built

- `src/nirvana_accounts.rs`:
  - `accounts_path`, `read_chosen`, and `write_chosen` managing `<home>/nirvana/accounts.json` with `{"antigravity": "<email>"}` through a temporary file and rename.
  - `record_after_login` invoked on `login(Cli::Antigravity)` completion when ending signed in, never when signed out, and ignoring other CLIs.
  - `drift_ruling(chosen, current, app_running)` pure function implementing drift logic without side-effects: matching or missing choices/logs evaluate to `None`; different accounts evaluate to `Some(Drift)`.
  - `cause_line(chosen, app_running)` generating explicit rationale: `"The Antigravity app put this account back. Sign the app in as <chosen> too."` when `Antigravity.exe` is running, and `"agy went back to this account. Press switch to sign in again."` otherwise.
  - `is_antigravity_running()` reusing the Toolhelp snapshot process walk (`crate::nirvana_servers::read::processes()`).
  - Cache management: `DRIFT` mutex, `DRIFT_CHECK_EVERY` (60s) background polling in the worker thread, and `antigravity_drift()` read-only accessor on the draw path.
  - 5 unit tests verifying all ruling combinations, cause line texts, log directory reading, and atomic file creation.
- `src/client/shell/nirvana_accounts.rs`:
  - Widened `ACCOUNTS_WIDTH` from 72 to 104 so unclipped rows and cause lines fit comfortably on wide screens.
  - `account_drift_text(current, drift, room)` responsive formatting: wide `fahim.private@gmail.com ⚠ chosen personal.fahim.amin@gmail.com`, dropping `"chosen"` then chosen email as room shrinks, keeping `⚠`.
  - `row_text_with_drift` and `row_text` fallback.
  - `render_accounts_panel` drawing drifted Antigravity row in `palette.yellow`, keeping button `[ switch ]`, and adding cause line directly under the row in `muted` style.
- `src/client/shell/nirvana_footer.rs`:
  - `render_footer` drawing warning mark ` ⚠` in `palette.yellow` beside `accounts` on the foot button when `antigravity_drift().is_some()`.
  - Preserved exact find text for `accounts_foot_button` control (`*accounts = Rect::new(area.x, button_y, button_width + 1, 1);`).
- `src/client/shell/tests/nirvana_footer.rs`:
  - 4 client integration tests verifying wide/narrow row formatting, cause lines with and without app running, warning colors, and foot button mark.
- `nirvana/controls.d/p18.py`:
  - 18 sabotage controls covering every assertion in Plan 18.
