## What a gate did with a blocked delivery before this package (read by hand)

`gate()` routes a blocked plain order to `gate_subject`, which runs every deterministic axis (rebase, tests, report,
owned, sources, served). `report_axis` fails any status but `done`: "the worker reported blocked: <summary>". A
failing axis goes to `send_back`, which seats `<root>-s<n>` on the rule's pick (or one seat up for invented videos)
with the brief "fix what the gate found". So a gate by hand of a blocked order paid for a redo of a worker that said
it cannot go on, and the block's question reached no one but the redo. It could never pass (the report axis fails).

## Which wins when a gate fail and a block both apply

**The block.** A blocked delivery with invented sources, an unowned file or a rebase conflict is asked, not sent back
and not stepped up. Why: every send-back is the same order again with "fix what the gate found" (or, for invented
sources, "do the order again from the start"). The worker's report says the order cannot go on as written; a redo
meets the same block and pays for it, and the question in the block is answered by no one. The gate's findings are
not lost: they are in the question after the block, in the `Gated` event's axes, in the seat's tally (an `asked`
counts as a failure), and in the lesson/card counters (`note_gated(..., false)`). The orchestrator sees the block
three times in its queue: `o delivered blocked: <summary>`, `o gated asked: report: the worker reported blocked:
<summary>`, and `o asks: o is blocked: <summary>...`.
