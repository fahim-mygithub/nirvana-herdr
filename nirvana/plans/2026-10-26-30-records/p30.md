## The plan was wrong here

1. **Indentation against the ledger's example at the default width.** At `sidebar_width: 26` an agent row has 19
   text cells after its icon (plan 26 record). `v8-build S5.5 1m07s` is exactly 19 cells. Any indent past the
   agents' text column cuts the owner's own example (`v8-bu… S5.5 1m07s` at two cells in). The builder put the dim
   tree marker in the agents' icon column and the text in the agents' text column. The `├`/`└` glyphs carry the
   "under the space's agents" reading. A worktree space has 14 text cells, so its names are cut.
2. **Separator.** The panel's live row puts two spaces between model and elapsed, on its second line. The spaces
   row uses one space, as in the plan's own example `v8-build S5.5 1m07s`.
3. **"No new reads, no new polling" (§2) is not kept, by the review round's decision.** That line was the
   orchestrator's wording; the owner's words are "have only the active agents ... be shown in the spacess vieww". The
   build read the panel's cache without asking (`peek`), so a pane the panel did not ask for was dropped by the
   poller after 60 s and its rows left while the worker ran. The spaces view now asks for its own spaces' agent panes,
   as the panel does, so the poller keeps reading them (every 2 s while a worker runs, 10 s otherwise) as long as the
   space is laid out. A pane nobody asks for is still dropped after 60 s. It also passes the pane's folder and session
   to the poller (`update_pane_query`), because a pane the panel never listed has no folder and would never be read.

## Not done, unsure

- A worker row has no click target. A click on it selects the space, as the rest of the space's rectangle does. The
  plan did not ask for one.
- The review's "panel not drawn" case (a short window, a dragged divider) is not reachable in `render_sidebar`: the
  divider's clamp leaves the lower section at least 3 rows when the sidebar has 6 or more, and under 6 the spaces
  body has no rows. The case that is reachable is a filtered agents view (`agent_view_label`), whose `agent_order`
  can leave out a space's panes; the F1 test uses it.
- The spaces view asks for the panes of every laid-out space, including spaces scrolled out of view, because their
  worker rows count in the list's scroll height. A collapsed group's children are not laid out and are not asked for.
- A seat worker running in a herdr pane of the same space still shows as its own agent row (`space_agents` never
  filtered seat worker panes). It is the same as before this plan, and is left unchanged.
- (Superseded by review F1: `peek` is gone and `p30_spaces_view_asks` controls the asking.) That `sidebar.rs` used
  `peek` and not `cached` had no control. The tests set the view through `TEST_VIEW`, which
  both read.
- Live check for the owner, plan §4: start a run, watch the row under the space while the worker runs, and see it
  leave when the worker finishes. Also (review F1): pick a filtered agents view that leaves the run's pane out of
  the panel, wait past a minute, and see the row still there and its time still moving.
