# P39u record: phone uploads that never landed, and a failed one that said nothing

Branch `p39-upload-fix`, from master fbb27a5e (alpha 0.58.1). No version bump, no CHANGELOG: the merge does those.

## The incident, 2026-11-06 ~01:55 local (06:55Z), the owner's iPhone, 0.58.1

The owner: "The app kind of works there is a few bugs alerts work but if I have attachments and try to send the button
does work" (meaning: does not). Their screenshot, on the chat of an idle Claude in space `3D-Printer-Flow-xtool`: two
chips, `image.jpg · 2.7 MB` in the failed colour and `image.jpg · uploading`; the note "Wait for the upload to finish.";
a typed message. The status bar shows one cellular bar **and Wi-Fi** (iOS shows both; the brief said cellular).

Read on the PC (read only):

| What | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| `.nirvana/` in the space | existed since 2026-10-23 |
| `.nirvana/uploads/` | **created** 01:55:36.653 local; **last changed 01:59:19.733**, 223 s later |
| `.nirvana/uploads/.gitignore` | created 01:55:36.653, 2 bytes |
| files in `uploads/` | only `.gitignore`: no upload, no `.partial` |
| `herdr-server.log` | no `nirvana_remote` line after 06:48:17Z: the upload route logged nothing, by design then |

So both uploads reached the route (past every check: the folder was made after the agent lookup), one made the folder,
and something kept changing the folder for 223 s, each time leaving nothing behind (`save` removes its `.partial` on
a failure).

## The causes

### 1. Proved: two uploads at once into a space with no `uploads/` race, and one fails at once

`send::uploads_dir` checked `dir.exists()` and then called `create_dir`. Two photos picked together start two uploads
in the same millisecond; both see the folder missing, one makes it, the other's `create_dir` fails with "Cannot create
a file when that file already exists. (os error 183)", answered 409. That is the red chip in the screenshot, failed
at once, and its reason went to the note line, which the next note ("Wait for the upload to finish.") overwrote.

Reproduced three ways:
- A real browser (headless Chromium through a stand-in for tailscaled's proxy, below), two 200 KB files at once into
  a fresh space, 0.58.1's code: the first chip read `failed: ...\.nirvana: Cannot create a file when that file already
  exists. (os error 183)` 2.6 s in; the second landed.
- `uploads_at_once_into_a_new_space_all_get_the_folder` (150 rounds of 8 at once) and
  `two_photos_at_once_into_a_new_space_both_land` (the incident's shape, through the server): with 0.58.1's
  check-then-create put back, both failed **5 runs out of 5**.

Fix: `create_dir` and take `AlreadyExists` as success (then the same plain-folder check), and the `.gitignore` made
with `create_new`, `AlreadyExists` taken too.

### 2. Proved in Chrome, not measured in Safari: a too-slow body answered 408 is re-sent by the browser, silently

`BodyError::Timeout` answered **408 Request Timeout**. A browser reads a 408 as "the server dropped an idle
connection" and sends the request again by itself. Measured with Chromium XHR through the proxy stand-in, a 200 KB
upload slowed to 5 KB/s (server deadline 10 s + 3 s): the server saw **four** uploads at 13 s intervals
(`front4.log`: 02:33:38, :51, 02:34:04, :17) and logged a 408 for the first, while the page's XHR saw nothing until its
own timeout at 44 s. With the body timeout answered **400** instead: one upload, and the chip read `failed: the upload
was too slow (63 KB in 13 s)` at 14 s.

The incident fits this: `uploads/` last changed 223 s after it was made, about four of the 51 s deadlines a 2.7 MB
body gets (10 s + 2 700 000 / 65 536 s), each attempt creating and removing its `.partial`, while the chip stayed
"uploading" and Send refused. *What is not measured: whether iPhone Safari re-sends a 408 on an HTTP/2 stream, and
the phone's uplink that night.* The 400 is right either way: the page hears of the failure.

### 3. Page defects (read in the code, then seen fixed in Chromium)

- `nv.upload` was a `fetch` with no end: a request that never got an answer left the chip "uploading" forever, and
  Send answered "Wait for the upload to finish." forever.
- A failed chip showed only its size, in red; why went to the shared note line.
- A failed chip could be removed but not retried; Send with one just sent the rest.

## Ruled out, with the repro

`repro/main.go` and `front/main.go` (private TEMP `C:\Users\fahim\AppData\Local\Temp\p39u`, Go 1.23.4 unpacked
there, not installed) rebuild tailscaled's serve proxy from `ipn/ipnlocal/serve.go` v1.102.2 lines 646-1023: an HTTPS
server with h2 (`hs.ServeTLS`), `httputil.ReverseProxy` with `Rewrite` (`SetURL`, `Out.Host = In.Host`, the
forwarded and identity headers), and its `http.Transport` (`ForceAttemptHTTP2`, `ExpectContinueTimeout: 1s`). The
server behind it is the real Nirvana Remote with its real limits and the fake herdr
(`p39u_harness_serves_for_the_proxy_repro`, ignored, run by hand).

| Case, h2 client through the proxy | Result | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.7 MB with a length | 200 in 0.0 s | framing is fine |
| 2.7 MB with no length (h2, so chunked to the server) | 200 in 0.0 s | chunked is fine |
| two 2.7 MB at once (space folder already there) | both 200 | concurrency, once the folder exists, is fine |
| `Expect: 100-continue` | 200 | ruled out |
| 50 MiB | 200 in 0.2 s | fine |
| 2.7 MB at 40 KB/s | 408 (now 400) at 51.2 s, 2 048 000 sent | the deadline works as written; reaches the client |
| a 15 s stall at 1 MB | 408 (now 400) at 15.0 s | idle cut works; reaches the client |

- **32-connection cap**: at most 6 live views (`stream::MOST`), plus the two uploads, is 8 of 32; P39b's
  `the_33rd_connection_waits_while_a_50_mb_upload_holds_a_slot` holds. Ruled out by count.
- **The `.gitignore` before the body**: written before the body is read; harmless, and the folder times are what
  showed both uploads got that far.

## The body deadline (item 4): not changed

`BODY_RATE` stays 64 KiB/s (10 s + 51 s for 2.7 MB). The brief's condition, "if the body deadline is the cause", is
not shown: the phone was on Wi-Fi, and no reading of its uplink exists. What changed is that a deadline miss now
reaches the page and the log with the figures: the log line and the chip both carry the bytes read and the seconds,
and a saved upload logs its size and time at info. The first failed upload on cellular gives the measured uplink to set
the rate from. A lower rate lets the owner's own phone hold a slot longer, nothing more: the body is read only after
the login check.

## What changed

- `send.rs`: `uploads_dir` without the check-then-create race.
- `http.rs`: a body timeout is a 400, not a 408; `Conn::elapsed`.
- `live.rs`: the upload route counts the bytes it reads and logs every failure in one line, through the refusals'
  throttle (one per kind a minute): `nirvana remote upload failed route="/api/upload" size=2700000 read=1015808
  elapsed_ms=10021 status=400 why=the upload was too slow held_back=1`; a saved one logs `nirvana remote upload saved`
  with size, read, elapsed. Never the file's bytes. The failure's answer carries `read` and `seconds`.
- `app.js`: `nv.upload` is XHR with `upload.onprogress` and a timeout of the server's deadline plus 30 s
  (`uploadWait`, the server's `READ_TIMEOUT` and `BODY_RATE` copied and checked by a test); it returns `{done, abort}`.
- `chat.js`: a chip reads `uploading 45%`, `sent, waiting for the PC`, the size, or `failed: <why> (<read> in <s> s)`
  with **Retry**; its X aborts its upload; Send with a failed chip opens "One attachment did not upload" with **Send
  without it** and **Keep it**; "Wait for the upload to finish." only while one is under way, and cleared when the last
  one ends.
- `app.css`: a failed chip's reason wraps; the Retry button.
- `tests_upload.rs` (new), `tests_live.rs` (helpers shared; the slow-upload test now expects 400 and the timeout's
  reason, renamed `a_slow_upload_is_cut_and_leaves_no_file`), `controls.d/p39b.py` (that test's name),
  `controls.d/p39u.py`.

Seen in headless Chromium through the proxy stand-in after the fix: a slowed upload failed at 14 s with `the upload was
too slow (63 KB in 13 s)` and Retry; Send asked; Keep it kept it; Retry landed it (195 KB); Send carried it. A second
run, Send without it: the text went alone and the chip went. Before the 400, the page's own timeout was what ended
Chrome's silent re-sends: the chip failed at 45 s with `no answer from the PC in 44 s`. A proxy that never answers at
all (`front.exe -mode hang`) was built but not run.

   agent's chat in a space that has **no** `.nirvana/uploads/` yet (or rename that folder on the PC first).

3. In `%APPDATA%\herdr\sessions\nirvana\herdr-server.log` there are two `nirvana remote upload saved` lines; their

4. A failed one: attach a large video (tens of MB) and, while it uploads, turn on Airplane Mode. Within the server's
   deadline the chip turns red with a reason (or `no answer from the PC in N s`), and the log has one `nirvana remote
   upload failed` line with size, read, elapsed and why. Turn Airplane Mode off, tap **Retry**: it uploads.
5. Make one fail again, type a message, tap Send: "One attachment did not upload" asks; **Send without it** sends the
   text alone.
