## Where the plan was wrong, or silent

1. **"reviews queued ... against the cap" is read from the journals, not the cap's own counter.** The counter is
   `nirvana_review`'s private `State` in `runs/.lesson-reviews.json`, and `src/nirvana_review.rs` is not in §3's
   visibility list. `queue` counts a review and then journals its `seated` event in the same call, so the two agree
   except when the journal append fails after the count (the lease is released, the count is not). That case would
   read one fewer on the square than the cap holds.
2. **Merges count as reviews.** A merge order has class `lesson-review` too, and the cap counts it; the square counts
   it the same way.
3. **"the counters agreeing with herdr lessons stats".** The square shows the live count (§1.1's own words), which
   is not a number `stats` prints (it counts every lesson per origin, decayed ones too). The panel prints the `stats`
   lines themselves, and the test checks the rows sum to them per origin. The owner's §5 check should compare the
   panel's Lessons head with `herdr lessons stats`, not the square.
4. **Ops in Today are the reviews' only.** A `profile-teach` order's card ops are not counted under Today; they show
   under Recent changes through the card ledger.
5. **"the cap read"** cannot be `nirvana_seat::table_for`: it writes `seats.toml` when it is missing
   (`read_or_create`), which opening the panel must not do.
6. **The project is the tab's folder, literally.** A tab opened in a subfolder of a project reads `no lessons yet`.
   `herdr lessons` uses `$NIRVANA_PROJECT` or the git top level; the square spawns no git.
7. **Repaint and painted-over panel need two files outside §3** (`src/client/mod.rs`, `src/client/shell/surface_patch.rs`),
   and "one panel at a time" needs a line in five other panels' toggles. Hunks are in the report; until they land, the
   square refreshes on the next repaint any other source causes, `take_changed` is `allow(dead_code)` outside tests,
   and the panel can be painted over by a pane's direct output.
